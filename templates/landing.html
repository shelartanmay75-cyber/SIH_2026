{# About APAARSetu Section #}

About APAARSetu

APAARSetu demonstrates how synthetic student records can support intake checks, identity investigations and academic credential verification. Identifiers are format-checked through a mock adapter; possible conflicts require human review.

The prototype also demonstrates capacity checks and selective sharing of signed credential claims.

Provides Ed25519-signed credentials checked by the backend through a QR-linked page.

{# How APAARSetu Works Section (Connected Circle Stepper) #}

How APAARSetu Works

A transparent, 4-stage governance framework ensuring record integrity from enrolment to credential issuance.

{# 4 Connected Number Circles #}
{# Connecting Line #}
{# Active Step Cards #}
{# Card 1 #}

01. Student Onboarding & Format Validation

STAGE 1

Approved institutions submit student admission cohorts via bulk CSV or manual registration. Every record undergoes strict validation against the 12-digit numeric format rule (^\d{12}$) and is automatically masked for personal privacy.

Result: Invalid or malformed identifiers are immediately detected before admission commits.
{# Card 2 #}

02. Dynamic Seat Ledger Capacity Check

STAGE 2 • PREVENT

The Dynamic Seat Ledger verifies sanctioned intake caps in real time. In a programme with 120 sanctioned regular seats, admission #120 succeeds; any subsequent attempt to admit student #121 is blocked with a CAPACITY_EXCEEDED rejection.

Result: Excess attempts are rejected; regular and supernumerary capacity remain separate.
{# Card 3 #}

03. Identity Graph & Cross-College Conflict Resolution

STAGE 3 • DETECT

Cross-institutional scanning matches academic accounts across colleges. Simultaneous enrolments (e.g. active full-time courses at VESIT and COEP) trigger explainable risk points (+40 duplicate ID, +35 simultaneous course).

Human Review: An investigation case is opened for review officers. No automatic student cancellation.
{# Card 4 #}

04. Academic Passport & Cryptographic QR Proof

STAGE 4 • PROVE

An authorised officer can issue an Ed25519-signed synthetic credential. Its QR code opens a backend verification page that checks signature, stored claims, revocation and expiry.

Result: Verifiers see a current backend result and only the permitted claims.

Security & Cryptographic Architecture

Protected by Flask-Login RBAC & Ed25519 Cryptography

APAARSetu safeguards the national academic ecosystem against unauthorized tampering, privilege escalation, and credential forgery through rigorous defense-in-depth engineering.

{# Pillar 1: Flask-Login + RBAC #}
Flask-Login + Granular RBAC
Secure Authentication & Tenancy Isolation

Every user request is authenticated and constrained at the server level, preventing unauthorized access and cross-institutional data leakage.

✓
Server-Side Role Decorators: Strict @roles_required server enforcement across AICTE_ADMIN, INSTITUTION_ADMIN, STUDENT, and AUDITOR. Front-end checks are purely cosmetic.
✓
Multi-Tenant Database Scoping: services/authz.py automatically restricts ORM queries. An institution admin (e.g. VESIT) is mathematically prevented from viewing or modifying COEP or VJTI records.
✓
Cryptographic Session Security: Signed session cookies with HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax protections. Passwords are securely hashed with Werkzeug (scrypt/PBKDF2).
✓
Brute-Force & Stuffing Shield: Thread-safe in-memory LoginRateLimiter automatically locks accounts and client IPs after repeated authentication failures.
ENFORCEMENT: SERVER-SIDE STRICT ZERO PRIVILEGE ESCALATION
{# Pillar 2: Ed25519 Cryptography #}
Ed25519 Cryptography
Asymmetric Digital Signatures & Verifiable QR

Academic milestone credentials and passports are mathematically sealed against forgery using state-of-the-art elliptic-curve cryptography.

✓
Asymmetric Edwards-Curve Signatures: Uses RFC 8032 Ed25519 cryptography providing 128-bit security, immune to side-channel timing attacks and collision vulnerabilities.
✓
RFC 8785 Canonical JSON (JCS): Payloads are serialized in deterministic, sorted UTF-8 byte streams before signing, eliminating whitespace and JSON malleability exploits.
✓
Zero-Network Offline Verification: Verification requires only the public key. Employers and universities can verify credentials offline without querying active internal databases.
✓
Mathematical Non-Repudiation: Tampering with even 1 byte (such as modifying student grades or award year) instantly causes signature mathematical invalidation.
VERIFICATION: ED25519 PUBLIC KEY TAMPER-EVIDENT GUARANTEE

Common questions

Is this connected to APAAR, AICTE, UIDAI or DigiLocker?

No. This is a synthetic hackathon prototype. APAAR references receive a format check through a mock adapter.

Does a shared contact prove two records belong to one student?

No. A shared contact or fee payer is an investigation signal. Officers review the evidence before deciding what to do.

How are credentials checked?

The backend checks the Ed25519 signature, stored claims, revocation state and expiry when a verification page is opened.

Can a risk score automatically revoke a credential?

No. Scores support human review and do not trigger automatic revocation.
